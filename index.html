Vehicle features designed for daily life with a Rivian.

Rear Display

The rear display offered little beyond seat heat and static climate status. I shaped its screen composition, proportions, and interaction model to give passengers a clear way to move between media, trip details, and climate.

Camping App

Camping controls were buried behind a settings toggle, making the vehicle's outdoor capability easy to miss. I brought climate, lighting, battery use, and power access into a dedicated in-vehicle app for everything from multi-day stays to backyard movie nights.

Initial release

Climate Schedule

Preconditioning supports comfort and battery health, but its benefits are easy to miss. I designed the schedule around familiar routines, so drivers can set cabin climate before a trip without needing to understand the technology.

Biome Lighting

Ambient lighting can feel decorative or distracting in a carefully designed cabin. I created restrained presets that let owners personalize their space while keeping the interior cohesive with Rivian's design language.

Pet Comfort

Leaving a pet in a vehicle can raise safety concerns for owners and passersby. I paired cabin temperature control with a clear, reassuring display to help others see the pet is safe.

The charging experience

For many drivers, a Rivian Adventure Network charger is their first interaction with the brand. I adapted the vehicle's design language into a kiosk interface that reads clearly in daylight, at a glance.

I carried Rivian's visual identity into a more utilitarian charging interface, designing task-focused screens around two physical buttons for clear decisions and fewer accidental touches.

Few drivers know their utility's off-peak schedule, even though charging at the right time can lower cost and emissions. I made that lookup a one-time setup so home charging shifts to the cheapest, cleanest hours automatically.
